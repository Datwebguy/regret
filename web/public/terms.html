The rules for using REGRET.

REGRET is a trading decision product. It is not a broker, not a bank, and not an investment adviser. These terms describe the software at https://regret.fly.dev.

Effective 13 August 2026 · Operator of the REGRET service

This page describes how the product works today. It is not personalized legal, tax, or investment advice. If you need that, speak to a qualified professional.

1. What REGRET is

REGRET helps you decide whether a trade you are already considering is consistent with current market data it can retrieve, rules you wrote, and — if you connect a brokerage — your real account. It returns a structured verdict: buy, wait, reduce, reject, or incomplete. It does not invent prices, balances, orders, or fills. If a required source is unavailable, REGRET says so.

REGRET never sends an order on its own. An order can be submitted only after you review a preview and explicitly confirm. The confirmation is yours.

2. What REGRET is not

  • REGRET is not a broker-dealer and does not open or custody a brokerage account.
  • REGRET is not an investment adviser and does not manage money for you.
  • REGRET is not a signal service and does not promise profit or loss avoidance.
  • A REGRET account is not an Alpaca account. Connecting a brokerage does not replace your REGRET login.

Brokerage services, when you use them, are provided by Alpaca (including Alpaca Securities LLC and its affiliates, as applicable) under Alpaca’s own agreements. You must already have, or independently create, an Alpaca account.

3. Eligibility and your account

You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter this agreement. You are responsible for the accuracy of the email and other details you provide, and for keeping your password secret.

Browser sessions use an HttpOnly cookie. Command-line and MCP access may use a bearer token you store yourself. You must not share those credentials or attempt to use another person’s account.

4. Analyzing without a brokerage

You may create a REGRET account and analyze an idea without connecting Alpaca. In that mode REGRET cannot read your portfolio or send an order. Verdicts that depend on account data will be incomplete or limited, and REGRET will not pretend otherwise.

5. Connecting Alpaca

Connecting a brokerage is optional. The supported method is Alpaca Connect (OAuth 2.0). REGRET requests only the scopes it uses: data and, when you choose to allow orders, trading. REGRET does not request account:write.

You authorize REGRET on Alpaca’s consent screen. REGRET stores the resulting tokens encrypted, tied to your REGRET user, and does not send those secrets back to the browser. You may disconnect in Settings, which removes the stored credentials for that environment.

An advanced, operator-oriented path lets you save your own Alpaca API keys. Those keys are also encrypted at rest, isolated to your user, and never shown again. Do not paste a shared or third-party key.

6. Paper and live

Paper and live are separate environments. The current public deployment defaults to paper and does not enable live order submission. A “submitted” order is not the same thing as a fill. REGRET reports the status Alpaca returns; it does not invent a fill.

7. Your rules, your confirmation

Trading rules, journal entries, and analyses you store belong to your REGRET account. Deterministic checks can fail or return insufficient data. An incomplete picture is not a buy. You remain responsible for every order you confirm, including size, symbol, and timing.
