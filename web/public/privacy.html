Privacy Policy

What REGRET keeps, and what it does not.

This policy describes the REGRET service at https://regret.fly.dev. It is written to match the product as it runs today, not a hypothetical future.

Effective 13 August 2026 · Operator of the REGRET service

REGRET is a decision product. Your REGRET login is not an Alpaca account. Brokerage data is requested only after you connect your own Alpaca account.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to people who visit the public site, create a REGRET account, or connect a brokerage through REGRET. It is written by the operator of the REGRET service. It is not Alpaca’s privacy policy. When you use Alpaca, Alpaca’s own policy also applies.

2. What we collect

If you only visit public pages, the host may see ordinary request metadata (IP address, time, path, user agent).

If you create a REGRET account, we store:

  • email address, normalized to lowercase;
  • a password hash (bcrypt). The password itself is not stored;
  • an optional display name;
  • preferences you set, such as default trading environment and whether monitoring is on;
  • content you create in the product: rules, analyses, journal entries, trade intents, approvals, and order records REGRET placed for you.

If you sign in, we also process:

  • a session cookie named regret_session;
  • the user agent string, stored with the session;
  • client IP, used to rate-limit failed sign-in and registration abuse. On Fly.io we use the trusted Fly-Client-IP header, not spoofable X-Forwarded-For.

If you connect Alpaca, we store:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, or API keys you paste, encrypted at rest;
  • environment (paper or live), connection method, granted scopes, status;
  • Alpaca account identifiers returned by Alpaca for that connection;
  • short-lived OAuth state used only to complete the redirect.

We keep an internal audit log of account and connection events (for example registration, session created or revoked, brokerage connected). Passwords and raw tokens are not written to that log.

3. What we do not collect or invent

  • We do not store your password in a recoverable form.
  • We do not send Alpaca tokens or API secrets to the browser.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party ad networks.
  • We do not invent market or account figures. Missing data is shown as missing.

4. How we use the information

  • to operate your REGRET account and keep you signed in;
  • to analyze a trade you submit, evaluate your rules, and show a verdict;
  • to read your connected Alpaca account and market data when that is required for the action you asked for;
  • to submit an order only after your explicit confirmation;
  • to prevent abuse (rate limits on login and registration);
  • to isolate one user’s data from another’s.

The public deployment does not currently have a language-model provider configured. If one is configured later, text you submit for analysis may be sent to that provider in order to produce commentary. Deterministic checks do not depend on a model.

5. Cookies and browser storage

After you sign in or register in the browser, REGRET sets regret_session: HttpOnly, Secure on the production site, SameSite=Lax, path /, lifetime 7 days. The raw value is hashed (SHA-256) before it is stored. The web app does not keep a session token in localStorage.

Command-line and MCP clients may receive a bearer token instead of relying on a browser cookie. You are responsible for where you store that token.

6. Who else can see data

  • Fly.io hosts the application and the data volume in the United States (currently Ashburn, iad).
  • Alpaca receives OAuth and API traffic when you connect or when REGRET reads your account, market data, or submits an order you confirmed.
  • Google Fonts may receive your IP address when a page loads typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com.

We do not share your REGRET account with other REGRET users. One user cannot confirm another user’s order or see another user’s Alpaca connection.

7. How long we keep it

Account, rules, analyses, journal, and connection records are kept while the account exists. Sessions expire after 7 days or when you sign out. Short-lived OAuth state expires in minutes. Login and registration rate-limit counters live in memory on the application process and reset if that process restarts.

Disconnecting a brokerage removes the stored Alpaca credentials for that environment. It does not erase analyses or journal entries you already created.

8. Your choices

  • You can use Analyze without connecting a brokerage.
  • You can disconnect Alpaca in Settings.
  • You can sign out, which revokes the current session.
  • You can refuse cookies by not signing in; the product then cannot keep you authenticated in the browser.

There is not yet a self-serve account-deletion control. To request deletion of your REGRET account and the records stored with it, contact the operator using the email on your account or the contact details on the Alpaca Connect application for REGRET. We will say what we can delete and what brokerage records remain at Alpaca, which we do not control.

9. Children

REGRET is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 to create an account.

10. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Session tokens are hashed at rest. Alpaca credentials are encrypted. Browser login responses do not include a raw session token. These are current engineering controls. They are not a certification, and no internet service is free of risk.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the effective date at the top of this page will change.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact details submitted with the Alpaca Connect application for REGRET, or the email address on your REGRET account. Service address: https://regret.fly.dev.
