# REGRET design system

Memorable thing: a trade is a brief you stamp, not a bet you swipe.

## Thesis

Trading software looks like a casino terminal. REGRET is a decision brake.
The interface should feel like a clerk opened a bound docket: paper, ink,
hairline rules, a red stamp. Not neon. Not Inter-on-charcoal. Not cards
in a 3-column SaaS grid.

## Type

- Display: Fraunces (optical size). Soft, slightly irregular, old-style.
- UI: IBM Plex Sans.
- Figures and tickers: IBM Plex Mono, tabular.

Never Inter, Roboto, Geist, Space Grotesk, or system-ui as the face.

## Color

- Paper `#E9E1CF`
- Sheet `#F4EEE0`
- Ink `#1A1612`
- Soft ink `#4A4338`
- Rule `#C4B79A`
- Oxblood `#8E2A24` — wordmark, REJECT, danger
- Forest `#2A4638` — BUY
- Brass `#8C6E2F` — WAIT
- Sienna `#7A3F1D` — REDUCE
- Spine `#241E18`

## Form

- Corners 2px. Almost square.
- No drop shadows. Elevation is a second paper sheet + a hairline.
- Legal-pad margin: a vertical oxblood rule on the left of the working page.
- Verdicts are stamps, slightly rotated, outlined, never pills.
- Numbers never fake. Missing values are an em dash plus "Unavailable".
